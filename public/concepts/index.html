PHANTOM ROSTER · LAYOUT CONCEPTS

PICKALAYOUT

Ten page structures, all in the Persona 5 style. These are clickable mockups with real units, card art and rotations (example crew: Kotone, Queen, J&C, Miku). The one you pick gets built into the real site.

01
Command Deck

Three columns: filter rail left, roster centre, crew + turn order docked right. Everything on one screen, built for desktop.

02
Phantom Stage

Your crew is the hero: three giant slanted portraits fill the top. Fits, turn order and roster scroll underneath.

03
Heist Plan

A step-by-step plan: 1 thieves, 2 navigator, 3 turn order, 4 calling card. One decision per screen; great on phones.

04
Battle Timeline

The turn order is the page. Big turn columns, each unit's move as a card; crew picker in a bar, roster in a pull-out.

05
Dossier

The roster as a sortable data table, like a case file: element, role, speed, tier, fit. Crew docked at the bottom.

06
Hand of Cards

Like a card game: your crew lies on the table, candidates fan out as your hand at the bottom. Mobile-first.

07
Pause Menu

A huge slanted menu of names on the left, the selected unit up close on the right with skills, partners and Add to crew.

08
Hideout HQ

A dashboard of tiles: crew, score, roles covered, turn order, best fits and what's on the banner, all at a glance.

09
Calling Card

An editorial page on paper: your crew as the front-page story, analysis in columns, the turn order as a printed schedule.

10
Versus

A diagonal split: your crew on the red side, ranked candidates on the black side with the reasons written out (no hover needed).
